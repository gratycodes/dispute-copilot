| check | result |

> McNemar's test assumes independent examples; with groups, rely on the cluster bootstrap CI and group-level permutation test instead.
> Minimum detectable effect ignores clustering, so it is optimistic.
> The bootstrap CI and the permutation test disagree at this confidence level; the result is borderline.
